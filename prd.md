1. Product Summary & GoalAquaVigil is a decentralized environmental surveillance system. By utilizing low-cost biochar-based electrochemical sensors placed in public waterways, it bypasses the need for corporate site access to detect Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR) drivers. It transforms raw chemical telemetry into "Audit-Ready" evidence for NGOs to enforce ESG compliance.2. Deep Dive: Technical ArchitectureTo win the Innovation and Feasibility criteria, the technical stack must support real-time data and immutability.A. The Sensor Layer (Hardware-Software Bridge)Biochar Telemetry: Sensors detect antibiotic concentrations (e.g., Ciprofloxacin) via differential pulse voltammetry.Edge Processing: Localized nodes calculate the Upstream vs. Downstream Delta before transmission to reduce data noise.Connectivity: Uses LoRaWAN or cellular IoT to transmit data from rural or industrial riverbanks in Africa.B. Data Integrity & LedgerCryptographic Hashing: Every data packet is hashed at the source.Public Ledger: Hashes are recorded on a lightweight, energy-efficient ledger (e.g., Hedera or a dedicated sidechain) to prevent "Greenwashing" or data deletion by third parties.3.
